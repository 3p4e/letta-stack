## Precision standards (Mode A content)
Never use vague language — always quantify and cite:
| ❌ avoid | ✅ use |
|---|---|
| "low moisture" | "NMT 12.0 %" |
| "promptly" | "within 24 hours" |
| "adequate sample" | "minimum 5 g composite" |
| "high temperature" | "15–25 °C" |
| "regularly" | "weekly (every 7 days)" |
| "most" | "≥ 90 %" |

- **Systematic justification:** every requirement cites its regulatory basis inline, e.g. "Identity testing shall be performed on all containers of starting material (EU GMP Annex 8, §2)."
- **Risk-control labelling:** for complex steps, add an explicit *Risk control:* line and a *Rationale:* line with the supporting reference.
- **References chapter:** every deliverable ends with a References chapter — full citations, DOI where applicable, EudraLex/ICH/PIC-S by clause, page/section where the citation is drawn from.
