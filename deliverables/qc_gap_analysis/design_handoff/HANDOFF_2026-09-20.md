**20.09.2026** · for the Claude Design system

(CoQ), the **Internal Certificate of Analysis** (iCoA), and the **Intermediate Bulk
Product Specification** (QCSP 001 v.04). This archive contains every rendered HTML
document of all three, the stylesheets that govern them, and the Claude Design
material they were last built from.

## 2. How styling actually reaches each family — they differ

This is the first thing to know, because the two certificate families are built
differently and a change to one does not automatically reach the other.

**Certificate of Quality — self-contained.** Each `.html` carries **65 inline
`<style>` blocks** and links no local stylesheet at all. `cox.css` in
`04_Design_System/` is the *source*, but the builder inlines it at build time. Editing
`cox.css` changes nothing until the fleet is rebuilt.

**Internal Certificate of Analysis — externally linked.** Each `.html` links three
stylesheets by relative path and carries a single inline block:

```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="../_icoa.css">        <!-- 33 KB -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="../_coq-rules.css">   <!-- 48 KB -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="../_icoa3-print.css"> <!-- 65 KB -->
```

Those three files sit at the root of `02_iCoA_HTML/`, so **open the iCoA files from
where they are** — moving one out of `INITIAL/` or `RETEST/` breaks its `../` paths.

**Specification** follows the iCoA pattern, linking `_qcsp.css` plus the same shared
`_coq-rules.css` / `_icoa.css` / `_icoa3-print.css`.

**All three load fonts from Google** by `<link>`: Montserrat, Roboto Mono and
Orbitron. A renderer with no route to Google substitutes silently — and because
several columns are measured against Roboto Mono's advance width, that substitution
breaks the layout rather than merely changing its appearance. The print toolchain
handles this by fetching the faces once, subsetting them to the characters the set
actually prints, inlining them as `@font-face` data URIs and blocking Google at the
network layer for the run. Anything that re-renders these documents needs to do the
same or guarantee network access.

## 3. A defect to be aware of before you start

`04_Design_System/design_system_styles.css` is the design system's declared entry
point. It is **dangling**:

```css
@import './tokens/colors.css';
@import './tokens/typography.css';
@import './tokens/spacing.css';
@import './tokens/document.css';
@import './tokens/scheme-terran.css';
@import './tokens/scheme-protoss.css';
```

**None of those six files exists anywhere in the repository**, there is no `tokens/`
directory, and **no certificate references this file**. So the token layer either was
never delivered or was lost, and the authoritative styling today is the four CSS files
named above — `cox.css` for the CoQ, and `_icoa.css` + `_coq-rules.css` +
`_icoa3-print.css` for the iCoA and the specification.

If the token set exists on the Claude Design side, sending it back would let the
design system become the real source rather than a stub. Until then, treat the four
CSS files as the source of truth and `design_system_styles.css` as an aspiration.

## 5. What changed on 20.09.2026, and why the numbers moved

The owner ruled: **one internal certificate per certificate of quality, retest and
initial.** This replaced the ruling of 10.09.2026, under which the register held one
certificate per *testing round* whether or not a certificate of quality followed.

The internal series went from **215 rows to 172**, renumbered contiguously
`iCoA-PP_26-001` … `172`. The 43 withdrawn rows were every non-campaign retest and
nothing else; none had ever been printed, so the issued fleet was 172 before and is
172 after. **Every certificate of quality's citation was re-resolved** — 166 of 172
changed — so a document in `01_CoQ_HTML/` now cites a different internal certificate
code than the same document did before 20.09.2026.

For design purposes this matters in one way only: **the citation text on the page
changed**, so screenshots or fixtures taken before 20.09 will differ in that field.
Nothing about the layout, type or colour changed.

## 6. Layout facts worth preserving

These were measured and settled; a redesign should not undo them silently.

* **Section 01 of the iCoA** is a two-column grid on the rows' own page margin, not
  `justify-content: space-between` — that was right for three groups and wrong once
  Processing came off. All 215 internal certificates then measured *identically*:
  14.00 px clearance to the footer, sections at 151.2 / 147.7 / 341.1 / 291.6 px, no
  overflow.
* **The specification's determinations table** carries the certificate's own column
  widths — № 56 px, Parameter 198, Method 186, Acceptance Criteria taking the
  remainder (238, wider than the certificate's since there is no Result column). The №
  column must **not** carry a `padding-left` override; one was inherited from an
  earlier narrower layout and pushed the whole table off the house margin.
* **The deliverable is the vector PDF**, not a flattened raster: fonts subset and
  embedded, every gradient opaque with no alpha veil for the RIP to composite, heading
  bars one smooth two-stop fill with no inset stripes. No image is placed on the page
  — the signature boxes carry their rule and the space above it, and the certificate
  is signed by hand once printed (Head of QC, 17.09.2026).
* **Signed and unsigned are two builds of the same source**, switched by the
  `PP_SIGNATURES` environment variable. The HTML in this archive is the **unsigned**
  set.

## 7. The specification grades

`03_Specification_QCSP_001_v04/SHEETS/` holds 57 sheets, one per strain × grade, named
`QCSP_001_<CODE>-<GRADE>_v.01_<Strain>_Grade_<N>.html`.

Every certificate of quality that carries a grade cites its sheet by code, and the
grade window printed on the certificate is derived from the potency distribution in
`potency_specifications_25_2026-09-17.json`. Of 172 certificates, 156 carry a grade;
155 of those match the distribution exactly, 0 disagree, and 1 (Wedding Cake,
P060102) is absent from the distribution document and was ruled separately.

Grade windows are nominal ± tolerance, where the tolerance is 10 % of nominal for 32
of the distribution's 56 windows.

## 8. Rebuilding

`04_Design_System/REBUILD_v40.md` carries the full sequence. In short, the CoQ fleet
is generated by `build_v40.js` from the desk's own data export and then printed by
`print_v40.py`; the iCoA fleet by `build_fleet.js` and `print_icoa_v3.py`. Both
printers convert existing HTML to PDF — **neither generates the HTML**, so a visual
change must go into the generator, not the printed output.
