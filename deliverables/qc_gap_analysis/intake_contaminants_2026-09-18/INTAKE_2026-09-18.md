# The contaminant intake of 18.09.2026

**Head of QC, 18.09.2026:** "There cannot be parameters for heavy metals not tested."

He was right, and the reason was not that the tests had not been run. Sixty-five scans of
the Institute of Public Health's contaminant panel — 29 pesticide residues, four heavy
metals and total aflatoxins each — were in `eCoA_DATABASE` unread, for lots whose
certificate of quality printed a dash against #11. Fifty-eight distinct documents (seven
of the sixty-five were the same certificate filed under two paths).

## The gate

Every value passed the desk's two-read gate: two readers transcribed every scan
independently, without seeing each other's work, and `reconcile.py` takes a value only
where both wrote the same thing. The comparison is on the printed string — "н.д.",
"< 0,01" and "< 2" are different assertions — folding only presentation: space, the
decimal comma, Cyrillic look-alikes, and the form's "*" non-accredited marker, which one
reader kept on the analyte's name and the other did not.

    documents read twice   58
    values agreed          1927
    held                   0

One row was read the second time by the desk itself: page 4 of `87/2026`, which the second
reader had not reached — 2,2 µg/kg total aflatoxins against 4, read after the first
reader's figure was already known, on a page that admits one reading.

## What was applied

        #10.2 total aflatoxins                 16

        #12 pesticide residues (panel summary) 22
    already printed, and the new reading agrees   394
    residue count corrected on #12                 79

The 79 are one correction: the #12 sentence said "all 25 residues" or "all 22 residues"
from a single read on 09.09.2026, and both readers count 29 on every one of those forms.
The desk counted a page itself to be sure. The result — ND, or < 0,01 on the forms that
print the LOQ — is identical in every one; only the count of analytes behind it moved.

Not taken: aflatoxin B1 and ochratoxin A (#10.1, #10.3). These reports carry *вкупни
афлатоксини* and nothing else. The desk's B1 and ochratoxin results come from Farmahem's
mycotoxin certificates, and on 52 release certificates the desk already holds that value
against a `197-`/`220-М/26` document of August 2026 — the re-analysis campaign, which a
2025 release certificate cannot cite. Those read "not tested" correctly; the retest
certificate carries the figure. Also not taken: *бакар*, printed on four reports with no
maximum, which is not a determination of the specification.

A limit column was read and never applied. The older form prints looser maxima (lead 5,
cadmium 1, arsenic 2 mg/kg) than the certificate's criterion; the result is the
laboratory's, the criterion is the desk's.

## What remains, and why

Heavy metals still untested: **25 certificates, 13 lots** (from 44 and 22).

* **OI-59** — two lots whose panel is in the archive with page 3 missing, the page that
  carries the metals table: `1065/2026` (SJ102501) and `3925/2026` (GG032601). The third
  such scan, `1625/2026` (J31122501), had its metals covered by another document. Each
  local copy is byte for byte the Drive file, so the archive's own scan is the short one.
* **OI-60** — eleven lots with no contaminant panel for the lot in the archive at all:
  eight with none, three with only a sibling sub-lot's, which does not certify them.

Eight certificates report a numeric aflatoxin result rather than "< 2": 2,1 (1057/2026),
2,2 (1059/2026, 87/2026, 1627/2026, 5700/2025), 2,3 (326/2026), 2,5 (1628/2026),
2,8 (1058/2026) µg/kg — all within the 4 µg/kg limit, all printed as read.
