ISSUE_iCOA — Internal Certificates of Analysis

215 certificates covering Identification A (appearance), Identification B (microscopy) and Foreign Matter — the three parameters examined on the dried bulk before the packaging process begins. Identity, dates and results come from the iCoA Register sheet of CoQ_Analysis_Master_v44.

Four sections: §01 identity, §02 one glass panel per parameter holding its method, principle, sample, conditions, acceptance criterion and result badge, §03 a per-analysis observation record (three columns × two rows of attribute cells per analysis), §04 the disposition. All labels are bilingual; the Macedonian gloss is set smaller and lighter throughout.

§03’s ticked option is drawn from each attribute’s conforming set by a hash of the certificate code — stable per batch, varied across the fleet. Six attributes have a single conforming outcome (bracts & stigmas, cystoliths + HCl R, foreign tissue, seeds, animal matter, mould) and are always ticked there: that is the monograph. Foreign matter reads Absent, with the mass field left blank rather than pre-filled with a zero.

23 certificates carry determinations made by the external contract laboratory; those state that attribution explicitly and make no in-house claim. Every gradient is composited against white and every shadow removed, so the A4 page prints and exports to PDF without a flattening pass.
