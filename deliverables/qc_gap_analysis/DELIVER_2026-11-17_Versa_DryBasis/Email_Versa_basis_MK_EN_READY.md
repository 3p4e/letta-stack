## English

Dear Sabina,

Further to your comment, we confirm how the THC values on the Certificates of Quality (CoQ) of the shipped Tranche 1 and Tranche 2 batches were obtained and expressed.

1. **Source.** The Total Δ9-THC on each CoQ is taken from the Farmahem cannabinoid analysis report of that batch (Tranche 1: 197-x-К/26; Tranche 2: 220-x-К/26). The laboratory is accredited to MKC EN ISO/IEC 17025 and the method used (HPLC/DAD, Ph. Eur. 3028 Cannabis flos) is accredited. Every CoQ cites the report number and date, so each value is traceable to an accredited report.
2. **Basis of expression.** As already communicated to you, Farmahem expresses total THC, CBD and CBN on an "as is" basis, without correction for loss on drying, following the calculation approach of Ph. Eur. 3028. The CoQ values are therefore on an as-is basis, not on dry mass, and the same basis is applied uniformly to all batches.
3. **Labelled assay.** The labelled THC of each batch (the grade in the product name, e.g. "24 % w/w THC") is the specification grade whose range contains the Total THC printed on the CoQ, i.e. the as-is value.
4. **Comparability of results.** A result expressed on dry mass is not directly comparable with an as-is result: the difference equals the loss on drying of the sample, which for our batches is about 6–10 %. Before LabCore's results are compared with our CoQs, we therefore ask LabCore to state the basis of its results (as is / dry mass) and, if dry mass, the loss on drying used. The sample preparation (mill, chambers, amount per cycle) has already been communicated to you.
5. **CoQs.** The issued certificates correctly transfer the results of the accredited reports they cite. If you need dry-mass values for specific batches, please name the batches; we will provide them together with the loss-on-drying value used and its source.

Please also confirm which batches/groups your comment refers to ("groups 2 and 3"), so that we answer exactly for them.
