**English**

Dear Sabina,

Regarding your comment: the THC values on the CoQs of the shipped batches (Tranche 1 and 2) were obtained from the accredited Farmahem reports and are expressed on an "as is" basis, without correction for loss on drying, not on dry mass. The labelled assay was determined from these as-is values, i.e. from the Total THC printed on the CoQ.

If you need values on a dry-mass basis, please tell us for which batches; we will provide them together with the loss-on-drying value used and its source.

Please also confirm which batches "groups 2 and 3" refers to.
