## The signature panel

> "from all iCOA please remove the QA manager signature ... and arrange the two remaining
> signatures like in the COQs." — Head of QC, 24.09.2026

The QA Manager's block comes off the internal certificate of analysis entirely — role line,
name, credential, date and rule. The internal certificate is a QC laboratory record: the
Analyst performs it and the QC Manager approves it; the QA Manager's review stays on the
certificate of quality, where it already is.

The two that remain take the certificate of quality's own geometry. Nothing in the design
system was edited to do it: `.approval-grid.cols-2` — `1fr 1fr` with a 110 px gap — is already
defined in `_icoa.css`, and is the same rule the certificate of quality uses. The grid is told
it has two columns instead of three. **Analysis Performed by** sits at the left, **Approved by**
at the right, on the same two column centres the certificate of quality prints.

The removal runs in `icoa_handoff/v3/build_from_register.js::dropReviewer()`, before any ink is
laid, so no signature is drawn for a box that is no longer there. It balances the grid's own
`<div>`s rather than matching a pattern over the document, and finds the reviewer by the name
printed beneath the rule — the rule `sign_block.js` already follows. A grid that does not hold
exactly three blocks, or holds no reviewer, stops the build.

Measured over all **172** internal certificates after the change: 0 name the QA Manager,
0 keep a three-column grid, 172 carry exactly two signature blocks — `Hristina Cekikj` and
`Blagoj Nikolov` — and 172 carry exactly two signature images.
